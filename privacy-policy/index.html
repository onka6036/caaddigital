Welcome to CAAD DEVELOPMENT, LLC ("CAAD DEVELOPMENT," "CAAD," "we," "our," or "us").

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

B. Project and Service Information

We may use collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Understanding your business and project requirements.
  • Preparing proposals and service estimates.
  • Providing graphic design, branding, website development, and other contracted services.
  • Communicating project updates and important business information.
  • Managing client relationships and business records.

CAAD DEVELOPMENT, LLC does not intend to sell your personal information.

Our website or its hosting infrastructure may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, performance, and user experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit certain websites.

You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies, we will update this policy and provide any consent or opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law.

6. Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information entrusted to us against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We encourage clients to avoid sending passwords, financial account credentials, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email or unsecured communication channels.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Fulfil the purposes for which it was collected.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may securely delete, destroy, or anonymize it, subject to applicable retention requirements.
